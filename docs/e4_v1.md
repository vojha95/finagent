# E4 faithfulness: sheet, judge and scoring rule (Phase 7.6; blueprint 10.3; D9)

E4 asks whether the insight cards are faithful: do the sentences a card quotes support its
explanation of the movement? Vineet labels every card on the sheet. An independent judge (a
local NLI model, D9) labels the same cards. The report gives agreement (Cohen's kappa), the
unsupported-claim rate and the share of signals that end as EVIDENCE_GAP (blueprint 10.3).
Parameters: `configs/e4.yaml`. Code: `finagent/evaluation/e4.py`.

## 1. Cards and selection rule (written 2026-11-25, before the judge ran on any card)

- **The final system's cards.** E3 (e3-0001) kept bm25 as the retrieval default, so the system
  after 7.6 step 1 is unchanged. The current code rebuilds the 69 cards of insights-0002 with
  no difference in text, status, quotes or scores (checked 2026-11-25; #283). `cards` reads
  `runs/insights-0002/cards.json`. It refuses the run if its as_of, signals.yaml, query
  templates or evidence manifest differ from today's.
- **Companies:** the 8 validation companies. Held-out companies are never in an insights run,
  and any company outside the validation split is refused.
- **Only quoted cards can be labelled** (VERIFIED with one or two quotes). EVIDENCE_GAP cards
  have no quote. R10 cards are model-derived and carry no filing evidence by design (#180). Both
  are counted (section 6) and never put on the sheet.
- **Rule:**
  - every quoted card of each company's **latest** 10-K filed by as_of (2026-11-24);
  - for each **test** company (validation without the dev companies AMZN and MSFT, D10), every
    quoted card of its **most recent earlier fiscal year that has one**.
- The dev companies' earlier-year cards stay off the sheet. The two worked examples on the
  instructions tab come from them, so no labelled card is shown with a label.
- Order: companies in configs/universe.yaml order, latest year first, signals in
  configs/signals.yaml order; ids E4-C01, E4-C02, ...

## 2. Sheet and labels

`eval/e4_labels.xlsx`, labels tab: one row per card. It shows the claim, the quotes and their
sections. The claim is the signal statement with its numbers: the card text without its
evidence line. The quote retrieval scores are not shown. The label is a dropdown:

- **supported:** the quotes, read together, explain the movement the claim reports (give its
  reason) for this company and year, and nothing in them contradicts the claim;
- **partial:** they touch the movement but do not fully explain it. They restate the change
  without a reason, explain only part of the claim (one of two conditions, or a related
  figure), or speak of another period (a plan for coming years);
- **unsupported:** they are about something else, or contradict the claim.

The card is labelled as a whole, on what its quotes show together. The instructions tab has
two worked examples with the builder's labels, which Vineet may disagree with: AMZN FY2024 R3
(supported) and MSFT FY2024 R3 (partial). `validate` and `import` work as in E3. Import
refuses a sheet with an unlabelled, edited, duplicated or missing row. It writes
`gold/e4_labels.jsonl` with the sha256 of the cards file.

## 3. Judge (D9; written before it ran)

- **Model:** `cross-encoder/nli-deberta-v3-xsmall` @ `a150876415327c80daeff35ca6f68f5ed8cf5c24`.
  It is pinned in configs/models.yaml: 7 files, 294,477,138 bytes, every file's sha256 and
  size. The model.safetensors and spm.model hashes equal the hub's LFS sha256. It is checked by
  `python -m finagent.retrieval.models verify` and loaded offline on CPU
  (`load_nli_judge`). The tokenizer is read from the pinned tokenizer.json, so no new
  dependency is needed. Evaluation only; the product never loads it.
- **Independence:** the cards were built by BM25 and fixed templates. The judge is a different
  model that never saw the cards' construction, and it is not the agent that built them (#166).
- **Rule:**
  - The claim is split into its fired conditions. Each condition is one fixed hypothesis
    (configs/e4.yaml `judge.hypotheses`): the movement in plain words, without numbers, for
    example `capex_to_revenue_change` -> "Capital expenditures increased.".
  - Each quote is a premise. A pair's class is the model's most probable label.
  - A condition is **entailed** when at least one quote's class is entailment.
  - Card label: **supported** = every fired condition entailed; **partial** = some but not
    all; **unsupported** = none.
- **Known limits, stated before any label:**
  - partial can arise only on a card with two or more fired conditions;
  - the judge tests whether the quotes *state* the claimed movement, not whether they give its
    *reason*. A quote that restates the change without a reason can be judge-supported but
    Vineet-partial.
  
  The kappa measures this.
- **Blinding:** `judge` writes only `gold/e4_judge_key.jsonl` (per card: the label, each
  condition's hypothesis, each quote's class and probabilities, the judge's pin and the cards
  file's sha256). The sheet never shows it. No command prints it. This document and the 7.6
  report do not summarise it. It ran once, on 2026-11-25, on the 27 cards (50 quote-hypothesis
  pairs).

## 4. Scoring rule (written 2026-11-25, before any label; run only after Vineet labels)

`python -m finagent.evaluation.e4 score --run-id e4-0001` (not run in 7.6). Vineet's labels are
the reference.

- **Unsupported-claim rate:** the share of labelled cards Vineet marks unsupported, with a 95%
  percentile bootstrap interval over cards (2,000 draws, seed 0). Also reported: his partial
  rate, and the judge's unsupported rate.
- **Agreement:** Cohen's kappa, unweighted, over the three labels, with a bootstrap interval. A
  draw in which kappa is undefined (one label only) is skipped and counted. The secondary
  measure is a binary kappa (supported vs not). The 3 x 3 table of Vineet vs judge labels is
  shown.
- **EVIDENCE_GAP share:** EVIDENCE_GAP cards / (quoted + EVIDENCE_GAP cards) of the final
  system's insights run, for every year and for the latest year. R10 model-derived cards are
  counted separately. The blueprint asks for this share to be "spot-checked". The sheet holds
  no EVIDENCE_GAP card (there is no quote to label), so the spot check is a separate, open item
  (#289).
- Both label files must carry the sha256 of the current `gold/e4_cards.jsonl`, and every card
  needs both labels. Otherwise `score` refuses.
- Outputs: `runs/e4-0001/` (header, metrics) and `docs/e4_scores_0001.md`.
