Generated by `python -m finagent.evaluation.label_audit --version v2 --report` from the hand judgements in `eval/label_audit_v2.csv` (first pass by Claude; the `reviewer` column is for Vineet). Pool: every validation row of `rows_v2` where M1 (`m1-tfidf-lr-v2`) disagrees with the tag-derived label, OTHER included on either side. Each row pattern (company, statement, label, label->prediction) is judged once and applied to all its fiscal years; a year whose tag check differs from the rest of its pattern is judged on its own. Validation only; held-out not read.

Classes: MODEL_ERROR (label right, model wrong); LABEL_ERROR (the tag or row-to-tag alignment is wrong); TAXONOMY_AMBIGUITY (the row is legitimately two items or the item definitions overlap); NEEDS_FINANCE_REVIEW (not decided without a finance judgement).

Tag check (latest dated column): AGREE = a tagged non-dimensional fact in the row equals the shown amount in magnitude at the displayed precision; AGREE_AS_SHOWN = equal only without the table's unit scale (a per-share or count row); DIFFER = none equals it; NO_FACT = nothing tagged in that row for that period; NO_AMOUNT = no readable amount shown.
