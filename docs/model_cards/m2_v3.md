## Known failure modes (hand-written, 2026-11-25)

1. **Clear key rows sent to OTHER at companies whose layout differs from train** (E1 e1-0007):
   MRK "Cost of sales" and revenue, ABT "Cost of products sold, excluding amortization of
   intangible assets" and "Acquisitions of property and equipment", in every year. The trees
   split on structure and magnitude features (position, subtotal flags, share of the statement
   maximum) that carry a company's layout; at a company laid out differently they outvote clear
   text. MISSED rises from 82 (M1 v3) to 121 fields; ABT field accuracy 0.811 -> 0.604.
2. **Flat grid:** out-of-fold macro-F1 0.786-0.797 over all 24 grid points, so the chosen point
   (128 SVD components, 15 leaves, 100 trees) is not clearly better than its neighbours.
3. **Raw probabilities over-confident:** validation ECE 0.040 (M1 v3 raw 0.029); temperature
   fitted on the train out-of-fold predictions (T = 1.328) gives 0.017. No calibrator is
   wired in (docs/phase8_v1.md section 3).
