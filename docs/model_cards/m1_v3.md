## What changed from v2

Same features, classifier, C grid and temperature fitting as `m1-tfidf-lr-v2`; the dataset
changed (`rows_v2` -> `rows_v3`, same 17,112 rows, 20 labels changed, 0 AMBIGUOUS), applying the
approved resolutions of the v2 label audit (`docs/label_audit_v2.md`):

- **Pattern G, concept map.** `AccountsPayableTradeCurrent` -> `accounts_payable` (META train,
  ABT validation; 10 rows) and `DebtSecuritiesAvailableForSaleExcludingAccruedInterestCurrent`
  -> `short_term_investments` (NKE validation; 5 rows).
- **Pattern B, label rule (BC14).** `pretax_income_before_equity_method` -> `pretax_income` when
  no equity-method income line follows on the income statement: CVX FY2021–25 (5 validation
  rows). AMZN, CAT, DE keep the separate item. No train label changed by this rule.

Train labels changed on 5 rows only (META accounts payable). C = 64 again (macro-F1 0.841 ->
0.845 at 16 -> 64); T = 1.296 (v2 1.300).

## Field-level result (E1, validation)

Field accuracy 0.905, company-bootstrap 95% CI [0.859, 0.950], 971 fields (BC13 headline);
strict (BC13 off) 0.897 [0.850, 0.944]. M0 on the same dataset 0.773 [0.660, 0.876]. McNemar
M0 vs M1: 18 fields right only under M0, 146 only under M1, exact p = 4.3e-26. v2 was 0.889
[0.838, 0.939] on 961 fields. Details: `docs/e1_validation.md` (v3 section).

**Optimistic.** Validation chose C and fitted T; the unit-line pattern B of issue #42 and the
audit resolutions were found on validation companies. The held-out run (e1-0009, below) is the
unbiased measure.

## Known failure modes (validation, v3)

1. **Same wording, different concept by filer (#28, pattern A).** "Total operating expenses"
   that includes cost of revenue (AMZN, CVX, T: 15 fields) and AMZN's pre-tax income before
   equity-method income (5 fields, F1 0). Kept as separate items by decision; needs structure or
   identities, not text.
2. **Operating cash flow totals worded unusually** (ABT, MSFT, NKE): 15 fields.
3. **Borderline rows flip on retraining (#49).** MRK "Sales" is back to revenue (v2 lost it);
   AMZN's long D&A label ("Depreciation and amortization of property and equipment and
   capitalized content costs ...") moved from P 0.50 to 0.44 and is now OTHER (3 more fields
   lost, 5 MISSED in all). Both are one-word or very long labels near the decision boundary.
4. **Per-filer wording** called OTHER: ABT income tax and operating income, MRK income tax,
   inventory, total assets, capex; CVX "Purchased crude oil and products" (cost of revenue);
   ABT unlabelled total liabilities and equity.
5. **Parent vs consolidated with NCI present:** T total equity (5) and T NCI net income (1);
   BC13 does not apply because T reports NCI.
6. **Unlabelled gross PP&E** (MRK, 5 WRONG_ROW) and **sign** (AMZN income tax, 5 SIGN, #27).

## Intended use

- Document-channel line-item classification for US 10-K face statements (IS, BS, CF) in the
  FinAgent pipeline; top-3 probabilities feed the Correlation Agent's re-pick (8.8).
- Current M1 of the model ladder (replaces `m1-tfidf-lr-v2` for comparison with M2 and M3).

## Out of scope

- Notes tables, segment tables, quarterly data, non-US filers or IFRS statements.
- Any row the row builder did not produce (statements incorporated by reference, BC10;
  div-layout filings, issue #31).
- Using probabilities as calibrated outside the validation distribution.
- Held-out claims beyond e1-0009: the held-out companies were measured once and are not
  evaluated again (BC36).
