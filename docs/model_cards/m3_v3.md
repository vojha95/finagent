## Known failure modes (hand-written)

1. **Clear rows sent to OTHER in every year, at one company each** (fields M1 v3 gets right):
   CVX short_term_investments (5 years), MRK cost_of_revenue (5), T income_tax_expense and
   net_income_including_nci (3 each). All are MISSED, never a wrong row.
2. **The differences from M1 v3 are row patterns repeated across years**: 13 (company, item)
   patterns, 9 in M3's favour and 4 in M1 v3's. McNemar counts each year as independent, so
   the company-bootstrap interval (which includes zero) is the more honest measure.
3. **Raw probabilities over-confident** (ECE 0.030), as for M1 and M2. No calibrator is wired
   in.
