## Field-level result (E1, validation)

Field accuracy 0.680, company-bootstrap 95% CI [0.399, 0.900], 941 fields; M0 0.626 [0.349, 0.854].
McNemar M0 vs M1: 29 fields right only under M0, 80 only under M1, exact p = 1.1e-6. The interval
is wide because two of eight validation companies (CVX, T) score 0 for a reason outside the
classifier (below). Details: `docs/e1_validation.md`.

## Known failure modes (from the validation error analysis)

1. **Not the classifier: no unit line.** For CVX and T the row builder finds no unit text, so the
   normaliser returns NO_UNIT_LINE and every field is MISSED (188 of M1's 291 misses). Issue #42.
2. **Same wording, different concept by filer.** "Total operating expenses" is
   `operating_expenses` for six train filers and `total_costs_and_expenses` for five; M1 picks
   `operating_expenses` for AMZN and T (10 rows), and the validation split has no
   `operating_expenses` rows at all. "Income before income taxes" (AMZN, CVX) is
   `pretax_income_before_equity_method` in the tags; M1 says `pretax_income` (10 rows, F1 0).
   Text alone cannot separate these; issue #28.
3. **Concept-map gaps make correct predictions look wrong.** 36 dataset rows "Property and
   equipment, net" (AMZN, CVX, T in validation; CRM, META, UPS, TGT in train) are tagged
   `PropertyPlantAndEquipmentAndFinanceLeaseRightOfUseAsset...`, which the concept map does not
   map, so they are labelled OTHER and M1's `ppe_net` counts as a false positive (15 of the 30
   OTHER -> ppe_net rows; another 5 are CVX's PP&E "at cost", a real error). NKE's BS
   "Inventories" (`InventoryFinishedGoodsNetOfReserves`, unmapped) does the same for `inventory`,
   and T's BS "Inventories" row carries `PrepaidExpenseCurrent` (label noise or a row-alignment
   problem, for the 9.5 audit). CVX and T "Long-term debt" (`LongTermDebtAndCapitalLeaseObligations`,
   unmapped) give 10 more OTHER -> long_term_debt rows. The labels also train M1 to call some of
   these rows OTHER. Issue #43.
4. **Header-only context and unusual wording.** Operating cash flow totals worded "Net cash from
   operations" (MSFT), "Net Cash From Operating Activities" (ABT) or repeating the header ("Cash
   provided (used) by operations", NKE) are called OTHER (15 rows); operating cash flow field
   accuracy is lower than M0 (12 vs 22 of 34). Rows that carry little text ("Redeemable preferred
   stock", NKE; "Commitments and contingent liabilities", CL) follow their parent header
   "Liabilities and shareholders' equity": OTHER -> total liabilities and equity (13 rows).
5. **Parent vs consolidated net income.** `net_income` vs `net_income_including_nci` is confused
   both ways on IS and CF (10 rows), and per-share rows under a "Net income attributable to ..."
   header are read as `net_income` (CVX basic/diluted).
6. **Unlabelled rows.** MRK's unlabelled gross PP&E total under "Property, Plant and Equipment
   (at cost)" (tag `PropertyPlantAndEquipmentGross`) is read as `ppe_net`, ahead of the net row:
   5 WRONG_ROW fields.
7. **Sign.** AMZN shows income tax as a negative "(provision)"; 5 SIGN errors, identical under M0
   (issue #27, Correlation Agent sign-flip, Phase 5).

Calibration: ECE is measured on the same validation rows used to fit T, so the "after" value is
optimistic. C = 64 is the largest value in the grid; a later check found C = 256 (0.819) and
1024 (0.810) no better, so the optimum is not past the grid edge. That check is not part of the
recorded run.

## Intended use

- Document-channel line-item classification for US 10-K face statements (IS, BS, CF) in the
  FinAgent pipeline; top-3 probabilities feed the Correlation Agent's re-pick (8.8).
- Baseline M1 of the model ladder: interpretable and CPU-only, for comparison with M2 and M3.

## Out of scope

- Notes tables, segment tables, quarterly data, non-US filers or IFRS statements.
- Any row the row builder did not produce (statements incorporated by reference, BC10;
  div-layout filings, issue #31).
- Using probabilities as calibrated outside the validation distribution; held-out calibration is
  not yet measured.
- Claims about held-out accuracy: not yet run (Phase 8).
