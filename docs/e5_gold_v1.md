## How this set was made (stage 1, 2026-11-25)

1. **Vineet's 70 questions** (paraphrases, traps and most clarifications) were written blind to the intent training templates (ff46372). They were imported with `e5_gold import`, word for word: no wording changed and no typo fixed.
2. **The 50 canonical questions** (Appendix D wording) and **every label** were then written by the builder (the coding assistant, which also wrote the training templates), **after training**, in `gold/e5_labels.yaml`.
   - The labels come from the blueprint's goal definitions (8.15, 13, Appendix D; #120, #135) and the grid cell only. The conventions are in that file's header.
   - **Limitation:** the canonical questions and the labels are not blind. Report the canonical subset separately; Vineet's 70 are the blind part.
3. **Order of steps.** No model (M0, M1) and no planner component ran on these questions before the labels were written. `validate` checks that each question names its company with the planner's issuer-mention finder, so both validations ran only after `label`.
4. `validate --stage labelled`: **0 label errors**; 10 question-stage findings, described below.

## Findings for review

**The issuer check flags 10 of Vineet's questions that do name their company** (kept as written).
- **What fails:** the validator uses the compiler's mention finder (`finagent/planner/issuers.py`), which by design:
  - matches "Chevron" only as written or in capitals, because a chevron is an ordinary word (`capitalised_names`): Q012, Q028, Q060, Q068, Q092, Q100, Q108;
  - matches tickers only in capitals ("msft"): Q030, Q094;
  - needs an apostrophe for a possessive ("abbotts"): Q017.
- **What it means:** these questions fit their cells. They are likely compiler misses in E5 (no company found), and E5 should measure them as they are. The compiler is not changed before E5 (#140).
- **Freeze is blocked:** freezing needs 0 validation errors. This needs a decision (issue log).
- **Resolved (stage 2, Vineet: option a; #214).** The validator now has its own company check (`CompanyNames` in `finagent/evaluation/e5_gold.py`). It never calls the compiler's mention finder.
  - Validation companies: names and tickers match in any case, and a possessive with or without an apostrophe.
  - Other universe companies: names that are ordinary words match only as written or in capitals, and tickers only in capitals.
  - The compiler is unchanged. The 10 questions count as real compiler misses in E5. In both runs, 9 are missed (NO_ISSUER), and Q100 (advice) is correct because advice is detected before the company is resolved (docs/e5_v1.md).
