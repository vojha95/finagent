**Cause A (4 values): absence from the visible channel is read as "not presented".** In the
complete run the missing components were read by the tag channel alone (AMZN current debt and
short-term borrowings, NKE and MRK current debt, CVX net income attributable to NCI: all
SINGLE_CHANNEL TAG_ONLY; the document shows no separate row for them). With both tag sources
hidden, no visible channel reads them in any filing, so the company-wide rule turns their NEITHER
gap into NOT_REPORTED ("the filer never reports it"; finagent.statements.build `never_reported`).
The derivations then leave them out as optional operands the filer does not present (flag
`OMITTED:<item>`, finagent.statements.derive), so funded debt is long-term debt alone and CVX net
income is consolidated net income minus nothing. For CVX the document channel also found no
parent net-income row without tags (net_income NEITHER, "Document: NOT_FOUND"), which is why net
income was derived at all. The same rule explains 10 of the 11 OTHER_REASON gaps (NOT_REPORTED for
items the complete run verified: MSFT, NKE and ABT operating cash flow and free cash flow, CVX
revenue and cost of revenue, MRK total assets, ABT operating income). These are safe (no value)
but their reason is wrong.

**Cause B (1 value): #114.** Under the untagged view the product-mode agent sees no tags, as in
E2 evaluation mode. T's I6 compares the cash-flow top line (consolidated, 23,386m) with parent net
income (21,953m) because no NI-including-NCI row is read, and the swap to the consolidated row
resolves it. This is the open decision #114 (recommended option (a): when NCI is not known to be
absent, I6 compares the cash-flow top row only with net_income_including_nci).

Options for the reviewer, **not implemented** (#147):

1. Under a view that hides a channel, never assign NOT_REPORTED (the never-reported rule needs
   both channels to have looked), and make a derivation with an optional operand that has no
   value a GAP (INPUT_GAP) instead of an omission. This also applies to a real untagged filer in
   product mode.
2. Narrower: keep NOT_REPORTED, but treat a derived value with any `OMITTED:` operand as

3. Decide #114 (option (a)).
