## Method

Each variant ranks the same text chunks of the same 10-K, as of the date, in the query's sections: **bm25** (k1 1.5, b 0.75); **dense** (bge-small-en-v1.5, query with the model's retrieval instruction, cosine of unit vectors); **hybrid_rrf** (RRF, k = 60, of the full BM25 and dense rankings, top 20); **hybrid_rrf_rerank** (the RRF top 20 re-scored by ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2, keep 3). The quote rules are the 7.3 rules for every variant: whole sentences of 6 to 60 words with an anchor term, not about an earlier year only, no contradicted change (#185); score = share of the query's IDF weight in the sentence; at most two quoted at 0.30 or above. Only the chunks read differ: the best 5 (the reranker: its 3). Candidate cells: **Q** = quoted; score; section; the chunk's rank under the variant.
