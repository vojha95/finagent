## Method

Clean document (no tags) read as blocks; Item headings found by `configs/retrieval.yaml` (a contents table is one block and never yields a heading; a contents list written as text is left out; the longest run in Form 10-K order is kept); Item 8 notes numbered in sequence from 1; text before the first Item heading is UNKNOWN, never guessed. Chunks of about 150 words (max 250) at sentence boundaries, never across a section; each data table is its own chunk. BM25 (k1 1.5, b 0.75) per filing over its text chunks. Dense retrieval, fusion and the reranker wait for D8.

**Rule history.** retrieval-0001: section rules written on the dev filings (AMZN, MSFT; D10) before any run on the six test companies. retrieval-0002: three general rules added after retrieval-0001 (#171, #172), developed and checked on train-split filings only, never tuned on a validation company: pointer Items 7 and 8 take their content from a later financial section (the pointer becomes ITEM_x/POINTER); a bare note number ("NOTE 1", "1.") with its title in the next block is a note heading; an abbreviation ("Co.") in a numbered note title is not a full stop; zero-width filler characters are dropped. **The problems behind them were found on test companies in retrieval-0001, so section detection is not blind for those companies** (the diagnosis below names them).

## Diagnosis of retrieval-0001 (written after the run; section rules not changed)

Every filing has every expected Item (1, 1A, 7, 7A, 8). The only UNKNOWN text in each filing is what comes before the first Item heading (cover page and table of contents: 517 to 1,657 words), never body text. No heading was left out as a text contents list or ignored as out of order. The test filings show three problems. The rules were not tuned on them (D10); each is logged for Vineet.

1. **Content outside its Item (#171).** CVX (all 5 years): Items 7, 7A and 8 are one-sentence pointers ("presented in the Financial Table of Contents"). The MD&A, the statements and the 30 notes come after Item 14 and before Item 15, so they are filed under ITEM_14 (35,134 words in FY2024). CL (all 5 years): the MD&A is in Item 7, but Item 8 is a 12-word pointer, and the statements and notes come after Item 16, so they are filed under ITEM_16. Effect: a search limited to Item 7 and Item 8 finds no MD&A text and no notes for CVX, and no notes for CL. This is also why both show 0 notes.
2. **Note headings missed (#172).** CVX writes "Note 1" as its own block, with the title in the next block; the note patterns need the title on the same line. MRK FY2021–FY2023: one note title contains a full stop ("Spin-Off of Organon & Co."), which the numbered-note pattern rejects. Notes must follow in sequence, so detection stops there: notes 3 to 19 (FY2021, FY2022) and 5 onwards (FY2023) are filed under the note before. A search limited to Item 8 still covers this text (ITEM_8/NOTE_xx matches ITEM_8); only the note-level split is wrong.
3. **Text chunks over 250 words (#173).** 28 of 14,605 text chunks, in CVX, NKE, MRK, T and CL. They come from (a) single sentences longer than 250 words (forward-looking-statement lists of CVX, NKE and CL; a sentence is never cut, by design), and (b) tables with no numeric cells read as text, with no sentence breaks: T's cover-page list of registered notes and its Item 15 exhibit index, and MRK's pipeline chart.

Also noted: Item 8 text after the last note (e.g. an auditor's report placed after the notes) is filed under the last note (#174). And a run of three or more body Items, each followed by fewer than 10 words, would be read as a contents list (#175; it happened 0 times here).
