## Notes (hand-written)

### How the balance sheet closes (not a plug)
- **Precondition:** I9 and I10 PASS exactly in the origin year. The opening balance sheet is the one I9 tested; its equity terms come from I9's operands.
- **Opening lines:** accepted reported values. `other_assets`, `other_liabilities` and `other_equity` are CALCULATIONs, each from its own side's reported total, and are held constant. None is ever solved as A − (L + E).
- **Double entry for every flow:**
  - net income goes to retained earnings and into CFO;
  - depreciation comes off PP&E and is added back in CFO;
  - capex goes onto PP&E and out of cash;
  - working-capital balances move against CFO;
  - debt and finance-lease principal come off the liability and out of cash;
  - operating-lease principal comes off both the ROU asset and the liability, and the payment is the period cost (prototype simplification, flagged LEASE_COST_EQUALS_PAYMENT);
  - dividends come off retained earnings and cash; repurchases come off other equity and cash.
- **Checks:** cash is the result of the cash flow statement. F1 recomputes A − L − E from the lines, and F2 recomputes cash from EBIT, depreciation, working capital, tax, interest, capex, principal, dividends, new debt and repurchases. Residuals are below $0.0001; they are float rounding on amounts near $10^11.

### Missing inputs (never zero-filled)
- **An item the filer never reports (NOT_REPORTED):** the driver is NOT_APPLIED and the line does not exist (shown as —). Examples: AMZN dividends (decision 4), finance leases for NKE, MRK, CL and ABT, and inventory for a filer with none.
- **A required input that exists but has no accepted value** (DISAGREE, IDENTITY_FAILED, NO_IDENTITY_CHECK, INPUT_GAP, NEITHER): the forecast is a GAP with the reason, and no numbers are shown.
- **A median with a missing year:** the median of the accepted years when at least 2 of 3 exist, flagged PARTIAL_HISTORY; otherwise a GAP (decision 3, applied to both repurchases and the dividend payout).
- **Payout:** years with net income ≤ 0 are excluded (flagged).
- **Tax rate:** when historical pre-tax ≤ 0, the 21% statutory rate, flagged; in a projected year with pre-tax ≤ 0, tax is 0, flagged NO_TAX_BENEFIT (decision 5).
- **Debt interest:** 4% DEFAULT when there is no interest basis or no average funded debt; leases at 4% DEFAULT.
- **Funded debt that includes finance leases** (CVX, T): the debt line is funded debt minus the finance-lease liability, so leases are never counted twice.
- **Negative projected cash:** allowed and flagged CASH_SHORTFALL (decision 7).
